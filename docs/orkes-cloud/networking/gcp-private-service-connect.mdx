# Connect Conductor Cluster with GCP Private Service Connect

GCP Private Service Connect lets you set up **private connectivity** between your network and your Conductor cluster, in either direction. Depending on your needs, you can set up:

- **Conductor cluster → User network**, so your workflows can reach private services on your side, such as internal APIs through HTTP tasks or message queues like Kafka
- **User network → Conductor cluster**, to remove public internet access to your cluster

Each direction is its own connection with its own setup. If you need both, set up each one separately.

## Prerequisites

- An Orkes-hosted GCP cluster

## Conductor cluster → User network

You publish your service using Private Service Connect (the side being reached) and share its Service Attachment with Orkes. Orkes creates an Endpoint (the side that connects to it), and the resulting IP address is what your Conductor cluster uses to reach your service.

### Get Orkes's project and network details

1. Contact Orkes to get the project number and network name you'll restrict access to.

### Publish your service

Follow [Google Cloud's instructions to publish a service using Private Service Connect](https://docs.cloud.google.com/vpc/docs/configure-private-service-connect-producer).

1. Under **Configure consumer access**, select **Accept connections for selected networks**, click **Add accepted network**, and enter:
    - **Project**: The project number Orkes provides
    - **Network**: The network name Orkes provides
    - **Connection limit**: The maximum number of endpoints that can connect
2. Confirm your service appears on the **Published services** tab.
3. On the service's details page, copy the **Service attachment URI** (in the format `projects/your-project/regions/region/serviceAttachments/name`) and share it with Orkes.

### What happens after

Orkes creates an Endpoint using the Service Attachment you shared, in the network and subnetwork your cluster runs in. If your Service Attachment is in a different region than your cluster, Orkes enables global access for the connection. Once this is set up, the Endpoint is listed as **Accepted**, along with the IP address your Conductor cluster uses to reach your service.

## User network → Conductor cluster

Orkes publishes a service using Private Service Connect (the side being reached) for your Conductor cluster and shares its Service Attachment with you. You then create an Endpoint (the side that connects to it), restricted to your own project and network, and the resulting IP address is what you use to reach your Conductor cluster.

### Share your project and network details

1. Give Orkes the project number and network name you'll create your Endpoint in, so Orkes can restrict access to it.

Orkes publishes a service for your Conductor cluster, restricted to the project and network you shared, and sends you the service attachment URI.

### Create your Endpoint

Follow [Google Cloud's instructions to access a published service through an endpoint](https://docs.cloud.google.com/vpc/docs/configure-private-service-connect-services).

1. Go to the **Connected endpoints** tab and click **Connect endpoint**. For **Target**, select **Published service**, and for **Target service**, enter the service attachment URI Orkes provided.
2. Select your network and subnetwork.
3. If the Service Attachment is in a different region than yours, turn on global access, and confirm with your Orkes representative that it is enabled on the Orkes side.
4. Confirm your Endpoint is listed as **Accepted**, along with the IP address you'll use to reach your Conductor cluster.
