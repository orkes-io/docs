# Deployment Guide: Orkes Hosted Cluster

With **Orkes Hosted**, Orkes provisions and operates your Conductor cluster's infrastructure for you.

## What you provide

When requesting an Orkes Hosted cluster, you'll specify:

- **Cloud provider**: AWS, GCP, or Azure
- **OCU capacity**: The capacity for your cluster.
- **Region**: Choose from Orkes' supported locations for your selected provider. Get in touch with your Orkes Account representative for selecting your preferred region.
- **Cluster name**: A name for your Conductor cluster which the developers will use for building workflows and agents.
- **Environment**: Production, Staging, Development, Test, or Proof of Concept.
- **Connectivity**: Internet access with authentication, or private access (VPN or peering required)

## What Orkes handles

Once you submit your request, Orkes automatically provisions everything your cluster needs:

- Networking (VPC/VNet, subnets)
- Kubernetes cluster and node capacity
- Redis (the primary datastore for workflow and task state, deployed with replication for durability)
- Postgres (indexing and search)
- Archive store for completed workflow executions
- Authentication configuration
- Conductor server, agent, and monitoring deployment
- Ingress and TLS certificates
- DNS configuration for your cluster's domain
- Health checks scheduled for ongoing monitoring

## What to expect

Your cluster will show a provisioning status in the portal while this runs, and you'll be notified once it's ready to use.

## Next steps

Once your cluster is provisioned:

- See [Managing Your Cluster](/content/orkes-cloud/managing-your-cluster) for actions like upgrades, certificates, and protection settings.
- See [Monitoring Your Cluster](/content/orkes-cloud/monitoring-your-cluster) to check on cluster health, DNS/cert status, and actions history.
- Head to the [Orkes Conductor docs](/content/quickstart) to start building workflows and agents.
